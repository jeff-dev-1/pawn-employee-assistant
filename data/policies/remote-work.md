# Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. The remaining two days are in the
office, and your team decides which days those are so that everyone overlaps.

Working from another country requires approval from People at least four weeks ahead,
because it has tax consequences for both you and the company. Approval is granted for at
most 30 days per calendar year.

Equipment for a home workspace is covered by the equipment policy, not this one.
